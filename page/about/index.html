About me

Know more about Diego J.

Leer en español

I am a Staff Software Engineer at Rebrandly. My main focus is driving technical stability and modernization in complex, high-scale systems. I am focused on reducing technical risk, improving maintainability, and enabling sustainable delivery.

Previously, I was Senior Software Engineer at Flywire, working in the Engineering Productivity team. Before that, I spent several years working at Telefónica, in Aura’s cognitive team. I am also the former head of technology at Bufete de Marketing (now inteliens), former developer at Drivies, and intelligenia (now part of Nazaríes IT).

I am passionate about learning, creating complex systems, and making software simpler, safer, and more sustainable to work with.

Apart from work, I like jogging, history and reading books.

I write this blog as a technical diary, with the aim of helping my future-me. I do not intend to create a perfect resource, nor an information resource for others. The aim is to create an useful resource for me. It is useful for you, good!
